# THUAI9 Celestial Chess Ranking Rules

## Upload and Matches

See the [Saiblo Wiki](https://docs.saiblo.net/user/user.html).

The AI you deploy in the "AI" section will automatically participate in the
global ranking. Saiblo will automatically arrange matches between your AI and
others, calculating scores. You can view the results of each AI by clicking the
"Global Leaderboard" section in the game. **However, this ranking is for
reference only and will not be used as the basis for final rankings.**

## Ranking Criteria

After the preliminary submission deadline, we will run a single round-robin
tournament using the last submitted AI of each contestant. A win earns 2 points,
a draw earns 1 point, and a loss earns 0 points. Rankings are determined by
total points. Contestants with the same score are ranked according to the
following rules:

1. **Direct Match Result**: The two contestants must have played against each
   other in the round-robin. If one won, the winner ranks higher. If it was a
   draw, proceed to the next rule.
2. **Net Wins**: Calculate the number of wins minus losses in the round-robin
   tournament. The contestant with a higher net win count ranks higher. If tied,
   proceed to the next rule.
3. **Submission Time**: The contestant with the earlier submission time ranks
   higher.

After the preliminary round ends, a certain number of contestants will be
selected based on the final ranking results to advance to the finals.
Preliminary round scores do not carry over to the finals. Before the final
submission deadline, finalists have two weeks to make modifications and
optimizations. After the final submission deadline, the same single round-robin
rules will be used for ranking, and the final ranking will serve as the final
ranking of the competition.
